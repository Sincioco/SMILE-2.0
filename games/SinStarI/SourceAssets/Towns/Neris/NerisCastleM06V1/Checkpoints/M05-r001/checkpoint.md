# M05 finish checkpoint

Source: source/neris-castle-M05-r001.blend. Final measured source: 251,729 evaluated triangles, 11 shared asset materials, one packed/portable 1024×1024 masonry albedo texture (about 5.33 MiB as RGBA8 with full mip chain). Geometry and material counts remain within the proposed 350,000/12 targets; these are not measured runtime limits.

The user-specified Neris - Castle - Front_4K.png was inspected directly. Finish includes layered pointed surrounds, lanterns, cornice corbels, tower and dome ribs, stone courses, crystal crowns, bordered banners, fountain and restrained flowers. Main volumes, court-before-palace arrangement, gate opening and bridge pivot are retained. No new large tower masses, interiors, bloom, emission or cloth simulation.

One flat compass/star + blue crystal master was reconstructed from the supplied original flag board and reused for 19 cloth banners/flags and 21 insignia placements. No board background, labels or motto were imported. Artwork is editable geometry in NC.Artwork.Emblem.* mesh datablocks; cloth has deliberate UVs and symmetric two-sided artwork. Static flat cloth was chosen for portability.

All 91 bridge poses and 422 route cross sections pass again after finish. Seven fixed neutral views and detail renders are real Blender outputs; earlier M03 clay render verifies the architectural depth. User waived approval stops, but this does not claim explicit human approval of dimensions or visual parity with the concept.
