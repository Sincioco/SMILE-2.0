# Reusable terrain tiles

These three PNGs are generated game-albedo atlases, created for this repository
from Sin's landscape references. They contain no downloaded photograph textures.
Greyglass-Strata supplies pale weathered sedimentary rock and quiet neutral ground;
Green-Slope-Tile combines meadow, moss and exposed rock; Snow-Slope-Tile combines
snow fields and exposed grey rock. All are reused through TownTerrainStyles.

The atlases were generated with ImageGen on October 2, 2026. Prompts requested flat
lighting, no objects or labels, and seamless left/right edges. Green specifically
avoids directional streaks and wood-grain-like repetition. The renderer selects
plain ground versus exposed slopes and varies coordinates at broad scales.
