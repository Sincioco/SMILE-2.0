# Sin Star I - Visual Storyboard - Draft 1

September 15, 2026. Full-story spoilers. All 47 main scenes and six optional scenes are visualized in 72 panels.

D01-D16 are accepted canon; see [Canon.md](../Canon.md). Dialogue and visual staging remain drafts. The D10 investigator lines are new adaptation dialogue. The full scene dialogue remains in the [v0.2 script](../Sin-Star-I-Game-Script-v0.2.html).

Read each sheet top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right. The [HTML reading edition](../index.html) places dialogue directly below every panel. Speaker lines below each sheet here remain editable.

## Page 01 - Room for One

**Prologue | Neris, night to morning / Earth, night**

![Page 01 artwork](../asset/images/archive/draft-1-sheets/page-01.png)

**Establish:** Show the loss behind Arin's isolation; connect a small nightmare to a distant listener.

**Growth:** Arin hides his need for others. Kael hears something he did not cause.

**Game beat:** Brief defensive dream tutorial; accept the waterworks contract.

### Panel 1 - C00-S01 - Close / memory

**Picture:** Inside a civilian spaceship, a mother's hand fastens a worn wrist strap on a young boy; his father's smiling face is reflected in the window, beyond which a sudden white flash begins. Tender memory interrupted, no bodies or gore.

**Caption:** Before the warning chime, there was a laugh.

**Mother:** Too tight?
**Young Arin:** No.

### Panel 2 - C00-S01 - Wide / lonely

**Picture:** Adult Arin wakes alone in cheap lodging, dark undershirt, armor on a chair. A cup slides by itself; a hairline crack climbs the plaster. He clutches the same ordinary wrist strap. An unused pillow is wedged into a rattling window.

**Caption:** The dream reaches the waking room. No one has taught him what his voice can do.

**Neighbor:** Some of us work in the morning!
**Arin:** So do I.

### Panel 3 - C00-S02 - Medium / dry humor

**Picture:** At a Neris repair stall, adult Arin in battered surplus white navy gold armor counts a few coins while a practical armorer points to his cracked shoulder rivet. Contract papers and a public water queue behind him.

**Caption:** Neris surplus armor. An empty emergency-contact field. One more contract.

**Armorer:** The rivets remember the first owner.
**Arin:** Can they pay for the repair?

### Panel 4 - C00-S03 - Wide / distant answer

**Picture:** Kael with long silver hair and black silver cosmic armor stands on a grassy Earth hillside above an ordinary warmly lit town. He turns suddenly toward stars disturbed by a faint violet ripple. Quiet concern, no attack.

**Caption:** On Earth, far from Neris, someone hears the disturbance.

**Kael:** That wasn't mine.

## Page 02 - Someone Comes Back

**Chapter 01 | Neris waterworks, clinic, relay and palace**

![Page 02 artwork](../asset/images/archive/draft-1-sheets/page-02.png)

**Establish:** Make Mira's compassion and political responsibility visible before ancestry matters.

**Growth:** Arin begins helping beyond his fee. Mira leaves with a mandate and a relief handover.

**Game beat:** Rescue a surveyor; isolate the unsafe water loop while preserving district supply.

### Panel 1 - C01-S01 - Action / rescue

**Picture:** In flooded industrial waterworks, Mira in plain navy travel clothes with tied brown hair kneels beside injured adult Arin. She raises a curved turquoise water shield against falling machinery. A trapped surveyor is visible behind a damaged beam. Her focus is medical and decisive.

**Caption:** A broken fastening. A collapsing conduit. A stranger who comes back.

**Arin:** That thing isn't finished.
**Mira:** Neither are we.

### Panel 2 - C01-S02 - Medium / care

**Picture:** Before dawn in a crowded modest street clinic, Mira washes a surveyor's wound while weary Arin carries a stack of water basins. Rolled sleeves, clean cloths, tired patients. He could leave but stays.

**Caption:** The recorder is safe. His paid work is over. He picks up another basin.

**Arin:** I'm not part of the clinic.
**Mira:** Neither am I.

### Panel 3 - C01-S03 - Wide / uncanny

**Picture:** Under Neris, ancient cyan glyph rings threaded through modern pipes react to unconscious Arin; dust hangs in air. Mira braces a ring of water around him while a frightened municipal engineer works the isolated valve. No spoken magical letters.

**Caption:** An old relay recognizes him. Shortages, concealed faults and Axiom pulses overlap.

**Mira:** Arin. Listen to my voice.

### Panel 4 - C01-S04 - Medium / resolve

**Picture:** Neris palace council: Mira now in white navy gold field armor places waterworks logs and a relief plan before her living king and queen. Arin stands awkwardly behind her; a severe marshal watches. Travel cases at door, local engineer holding the handover.

**Caption:** The princess follows the relay network after arranging relief and unfiltered reports.

**Mira:** I am the person you expect to rule the people drinking that water.

## Page 03 - The Man Who Came Back

**Chapter 02 | Veyra frontier port and evacuation route**

![Page 03 artwork](../asset/images/archive/draft-1-sheets/page-03.png)

**Establish:** Introduce Veyra as an existing canine civilization and Orin through protective action.

**Growth:** Arin learns that courage can mean staying to help without expecting to win.

**Game beat:** Hold the evacuation lane; escape with civilians instead of destroying the enemy force.

### Panel 1 - C02-S01 - Establishing / wonder

**Picture:** A bustling Kynari frontier spaceport on Veyra, amber cliffs and teal transit rails, low curved canine-built architecture, recessed scent-marked lanes, paw and nose operated kiosks, adjustable human counters. Intelligent quadrupedal dogs in tool harnesses work alongside human and diverse artificial civilians. A canine administrator looks directly at bewildered Arin; Mira beside him. No humanoid dogs.

**Caption:** Veyra: a Kynari trading world, with its own rules and people already running it.

**Port administrator:** Marked path, please.
**Port administrator:** I am still here.

### Panel 2 - C02-S02 - Action / impossible odds

**Picture:** Veyra terminal under raider attack. Arin holds a narrow approach while Mira treats evacuees on the east ramp. Massive veteran Orin steps off a departing lift and raises his broad shield beside Arin. A Kynari engineer restores the rail from a low service housing. Distinct hostile angular black sentries in distance.

**Caption:** The objective is survival. Orin gives up the last lift.

**Arin:** You can't win this.
**Orin:** Then stop trying to win. Help me get them out.

### Panel 3 - C02-S03 - Close / guarded warmth

**Picture:** Inside an evacuation shuttle after escape, broad older dark-haired stubbled Orin carefully bandages younger clean-shaven Arin's shoulder with permission. Mira checks survivors behind them. Clearly distinct ages and builds, quiet exhaustion.

**Caption:** He came back for a stranger. He was afraid, too.

**Arin:** Arin.
**Orin:** Orin.
**Arin:** That's going to be inconvenient.

### Panel 4 - C02-S04 - Medium / first belonging

**Picture:** Evening emergency camp outside a damaged archive on Veyra: Arin lays out three food portions, Mira leaves a canteen beside Orin whose hand trembles from lightning use. Warm lanterns against teal wreckage.

**Caption:** The next building holds both the records they need and the transmitter families need.

**Arin:** There was extra.
**Orin:** Convenient.

## Page 06 - What We Blame

![Page 06 artwork](../asset/images/archive/draft-1-sheets/page-06.png)

**Establish:** Let grief cause a specific wrong, then show accountability through action and apology.

**Growth:** Arin rejects collective blame. Zara keeps agency over the pace of trust.

**Game beat:** Verify records; reject memory erasure; repair the fault under Zara's direction.

### Panel 1 - C05-S01 - Close / devastating evidence

**Picture:** Zara cross-checks two civilian ship records on a station screen, times and hull silhouettes aligned without readable text. Arin grips the table, old wrist strap in hand, stricken. Mira stands near without touching him. The image of the lost ship is civilian, no cosmic conspirator.

**Caption:** A verified combat log identifies a specific artificial faction and unit. Zara had no part in it.

**Arin:** Is it the same ship?
**Zara:** It is the same ship.

### Panel 2 - C05-S02 - Two-shot / rupture

**Picture:** At a dusk station shelter, angry grieving Arin faces rigid red robotic Zara who sets verified records down between them and turns away. Space and a hard shadow divide them. No physical violence.

**Caption:** He knows who Zara is. He blames her anyway.

**Arin:** Like that unit. Like you.
**Zara:** What happened to your parents was wrong. I will not stand here and admit that it was mine.

### Panel 3 - C05-S03 - Medium / public correction

**Picture:** A human station chief reaches toward a mass memory-reset console while Arin firmly blocks the action with an open hand. Zara stands beside a distinct artificial maintenance worker. Fault logs and heater circuits, no threatening pose from civilians.

**Caption:** When the chief proposes erasing artificial residents' memories, Arin objects in public.

**Arin:** I know what that unit did.

### Panel 4 - C05-S04 - Close / no bargain

**Picture:** Quiet cargo ramp morning: Arin has placed a corrected report beside Zara and holds one side of a cracked tool housing while she repairs it. Their body language cautious, no embrace, modest beginning of cooperation.

**Caption:** He names the harm. She does not owe him instant forgiveness.

**Arin:** I blamed you for something you didn't do.

## Page 07 - The Second Dawn

**Chapter 05 / Part Two | Inactive relay observatory**

![Page 07 artwork](../asset/images/archive/draft-1-sheets/page-07.png)

**Establish:** Make Kael's compassionate motive and unacceptable wager understandable in the same scene.

**Growth:** Arin values particular lives over the promise of a perfect replacement.

**Game beat:** Hear the proposal and refuse; the revealed network route remains an evidentiary lead.

### Panel 1 - C05-S05 - Wide / tremendous power

**Picture:** In an ancient observatory, Kael calmly gestures toward a dead planet through an enormous viewing ring; a narrow warm band spreads across its cold atmosphere. The five companions watch, awed but wary. The planet is lifeless, no civilians erased.

**Caption:** He can warm worlds. He cannot rewrite existence's foundational rules alone.

**Kael:** I can address a crisis. I cannot make the laws stop producing new ones.

### Panel 2 - C05-S05 - Close / kinship

**Picture:** Close two-shot Kael silver-haired ancient-looking but youthful, Arin brown-haired surprised. A subtle branching succession of distant lives appears between them as an abstract light reflection, not a power beam transferring into Arin.

**Caption:** Arin descends from one of Kael's incarnations. He is his own person.

**Arin:** You're saying I'm you?
**Kael:** No. You are what came after.

### Panel 3 - C05-S05 - Medium / the unanswered cost

**Picture:** Mira and four-legged golden Milo confront Kael across an observatory table, Zara and Orin attentive. A magnificent but uncertain nebula beyond the ring. Kael's calm visibly strains as he cannot guarantee the existing people's survival.

**Caption:** The Second Dawn would risk the people already here for an untested improvement.

**Arin:** What happens to everyone here?
**Kael:** I would preserve what I could.

### Panel 4 - C05-S05 - Wide / refusal

**Picture:** Arin stands with all four companions, resolute rather than aggressive; Kael alone beside the open observatory ring, wounded by the refusal. A route of relay lights remains glowing behind him.

**Caption:** He asks again for understanding. They will examine his evidence, not surrender their answer.

**Kael:** You have one life with which to judge this.
**Arin:** It's the one I have.
