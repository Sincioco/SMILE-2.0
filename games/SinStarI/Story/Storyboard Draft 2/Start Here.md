# Sin Star I — Storyboard Draft 2

Extract the full ZIP, then open **[index.html](index.html)**. Keep all folders together.
No installation or local server is required. Images and movies are separate files.

## Explore

- **[Visual Storyboard](index.html)** — 120 individual illustrations, with dialogue
  beneath every image. The left sidebar follows all 53 script scenes.
- **[Full Script](Sin-Star-I-Game-Script-v0.2.html)** — the complete accepted reading
  edition with illustrations placed at matching story beats. Select any image
  to return to that exact storyboard panel. Both pages link to the movies.
- **[Films & Trailer](movies.html)** — three locally playable movies and their
  Unlisted YouTube links. Start with the trailer, then discover their story.
- **[Canon](Canon.md)** — accepted D01–D16 decisions and existing open details.

## What changed in Draft 2

- Preserved Draft 1 unchanged in its separate repository folder.
- Split its 18 sheets into 72 image files and added 48 new illustrations across
  all 47 main scenes, including an additional archive beat.
- Retained original sheets in `asset/images/archive/draft-1-sheets`.
- Corrected the queen illustration and reviewed new art for character identity,
  costume, anatomy and scene continuity.
- Matched the storyboard sidebar and illustration sequence to the full script.
- Preserved all script prose, dialogue and accepted canon.
- Added the three movies, creator/project credits and Unlisted sharing links.

Images live in `asset/images`; movies live in `asset/videos`. The story film's
local edition is 720p for a practical package size. Its 1080p master is on YouTube
and remains in the separate `Sin Star I - Draft 2 Movies` output folder. Both short
movies in this ZIP are 1080p. YouTube rounds the 30.020-second trailer display to 0:31.

The `Storyboard-Draft-1` folder retains original editable panel notes and art
provenance; its sheet links point to the archive. `production` holds Draft 2
illustration prompts, scene mapping, movie prompts, ComfyUI workflows and validation.
Production scripts are optional; they are not needed to view the website.
