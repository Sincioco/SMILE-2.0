Canon & Draft Notes

All 47 main scenes and six optional scenes are represented in 72 panels. The comic condenses the script; dialogue may be shortened, and connective captions and the revised D10 investigator lines are newly drafted. Select a scene number to read its complete dialogue.

D01–D16 are accepted canon. Dialogue, costumes, environment designs, camera staging and suggested encounters remain draft material. The artwork does not change the canonical character models.

Accepted Decisions

DecisionCanon
D01Arin's opening is in Neris on an unnamed inhabited world. Kael detects his remote resonance while visiting Earth.
D02Arin wears inexpensive Neris surplus armor; its resemblance to service equipment does not give him a military history.
D03A damaged ancient relay supports Neris's water and energy systems. Mismanagement, shortages, concealed faults and Axiom disturbances overlap.
D04Mira leaves with a limited investigative mandate and a relief handover.
D05Veyra is a Kynari frontier/trading world, not their hidden ancestral homeworld. Its evacuation introduces Orin, followed by Zara and Milo.
D06Wearable devices translate Kynari language; they do not create intelligence. Kynari spatial/scent engineering gives Milo skills distinct from Zara's.
D07Elemental arts are limited expressions of the Axiom. Living authority, coherence and deliberate control matter; sound recordings cannot reproduce Arin's effects.
D08Verified war records identify a particular artificial combat faction and unit behind the ship attack. Zara has no personal connection to it.
D09Use the home village and East Valley labels. Orin was an unwitting beneficiary. His exact age, relatives' names and his father's prior knowledge remain open.
D10Orin's father was never prosecuted because prosecution would expose deeper corruption within the military. The present inquiry exposes suppression. Reform begins but is not completed by a boss fight. Suppression does not acquit or absolve the father.
D11Ancient archives establish imported human ancestral patterns, consciousness participating in reality and persistent traces after death. This is fictional cosmology. It does not establish routine resurrection.
D12Earlier renewals were iterations of one continuing experiment and preserved Kael. His first physical return triggers a different termination condition that would discard him too.
D13Access needs two distinct compatible living signatures, not agreement. Rewriting foundations requires sustained cooperation. Arin's protective counter-Axiom accidentally completes access.
D14A pretested synchronized field aligns the party with elapsed time at home. It neither freezes the universe nor rewinds departure.
D15The party severs Aevos's destructive supervisory access while preserving the independent sustaining machinery.
D16Arin survives. Kael leaves the Origin alive but critically wounded, with no promised eventual recovery. Arin's lifespan remains unknown. The immediate-death alternative is a superseded comparison.

Still Open

  • Kael's eventual prognosis and capacities; Arin's lifespan and future development.
  • The old man's identity and the mechanism behind Orin's restorative lightning strike.
  • What Orin's father knew before diverting supplies; the original exposure route, individual suppressors, threats, correspondence arrangements and his mother's knowledge.
  • Mira's parents' exact responsibility and the unfinished course of reform.
  • Finer limits of conscious participation, surviving traces and the phrase half human.
  • Kael's exact Earth/Mars origin, remaining names and the Axiom's spoken form.
  • Q14's exact bodily fate for Aevos: the storyboard follows v0.1's staged death; acceptance of D15 settles the safe separation of control, not every Q14 detail.
  • Detailed gameplay, casting, performance and final art. Marriage, a child and later power development remain reserved for Sin Star II.
