Board measurements

FieldWidth = 700, GridWidth = 35, and CellSize = 20 agree because 35 × 20 = 700.

Timing boundaries

StartDelay = 100 is the early move delay. MinimumDelay = 45 prevents the snake from becoming too fast.

Why exactly 840 slots?

The board contains 35 × 24 = 840 squares. In the most extreme case, the snake could occupy every square.

What should FieldWidth / CellSize equal?

700 / 20 = 35, the number of grid columns.
