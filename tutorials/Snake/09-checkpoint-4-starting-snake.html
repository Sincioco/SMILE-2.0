Build the Starting Snake

The snake begins with five segments in one horizontal row. The matching arrays describe each square.

Length = 5Head at index 0Facing right
4

Paste the ResetSnakeBody routine

Paste below SpawnFood.

Checkpoint 4 — exact official code
Sub ResetSnakeBody()

    Length = 5
    Direction = KEY_RIGHT
    NextDirection = KEY_RIGHT
    SnakeX[0] = 17
    SnakeY[0] = 12
    SnakeX[1] = 16
    SnakeY[1] = 12
    SnakeX[2] = 15
    SnakeY[2] = 12
    SnakeX[3] = 14
    SnakeY[3] = 12
    SnakeX[4] = 13
    SnakeY[4] = 12

End Sub

Read one coordinate pair together

SnakeX[0] = 17 and SnakeY[0] = 12 place the head in column 17, row 12. Index 1 is at (16,12), then (15,12), (14,12), and (13,12).

Why two direction variables?

Direction is the direction being used now. NextDirection is the player’s latest safe request. Separating “now” from “next” prevents confusing mid-move changes.

Which way is the snake facing?

Right. The head is to the right of the body, and both direction variables are KEY_RIGHT.

Repetition builds memory

Reinforce what you learned

Say it again

The starting snake is five coordinate pairs. Segment 0 is the head; larger indexes move toward the tail.

Recall it

What coordinate is stored for the starting head?

Check your answer

(17, 12). Later, drawing converts that grid coordinate into pixels.
