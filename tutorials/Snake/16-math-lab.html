Scheduling instead of sleeping

The game does not pause the whole window. It checks whether TIMER() has reached NextMoveTime.

Current clockNextMoveTimeDecision
6,0756,100Do not move yet.
6,1056,100Move now, then schedule the next time.
At score 49, what is Score / 50 in whole-number math?

0. At score 50 it becomes 1, which starts the next speed band.
